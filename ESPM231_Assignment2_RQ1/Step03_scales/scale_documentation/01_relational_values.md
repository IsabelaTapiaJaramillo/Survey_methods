# Scale documentation — Relational Values (RV)

*Status: **validated scale from the Step 03 reference table**, used with minimal adaptation (translation only).*

- **Construct name:** Relational values: values that link people and nature through relationships of kinship, care, responsibility, identity and a good life (Chan et al., 2016). This is distinct from intrinsic and instrumental values, and from the NEP.
- **Source / citation:** Klain, S. C., Olmsted, P., Chan, K. M. A., & Satterfield, T. (2017). *PLOS ONE*, *12*(8), e0183962. Listed as **RV** in `scales_reference_table.md` (6 items; 5-point Likert; cross-validated in the US and Costa Rica).
- **Items (numbered):** These are the six relational statements administered in all three Klain et al. samples (Table 1: *comm, health, iden, kin, wild, other*). The seventh statement, *resp* (land stewardship), was given only to farmers and tourists, so it is not used.

| # | Variable | English (Klain et al., 2017, verbatim) | Spanish draft (to be finalized by translation / back-translation) |
|---|---|---|---|
| RV1 | `rv_comm` | There are landscapes that say something about who we are as a community, a people. | Hay paisajes que dicen algo sobre quiénes somos como comunidad, como pueblo. |
| RV2 | `rv_health` | My health or the health of my family is related one way or another to the natural environment. | Mi salud o la salud de mi familia está relacionada, de una u otra forma, con el medio natural. |
| RV3 | `rv_iden` | I have strong feelings about nature (including all plants, animals, the land, etc.); these views are part of who I am and how I live my life. | Tengo sentimientos fuertes hacia la naturaleza (incluidas todas las plantas, los animales, la tierra, etc.); estos sentimientos son parte de quién soy y de cómo vivo mi vida. |
| RV4 | `rv_kin` | Plants and animals, as part of the interdependent web of life, are like "kin" or family to me, so how we treat them matters. | Las plantas y los animales, como parte de la red de la vida de la que todos dependemos, son como mi familia; por eso importa cómo los tratamos. |
| RV5 | `rv_wild` | I often think of some wild places whose fate I care about and strive to protect, even though I may never see them myself. | A menudo pienso en algunos lugares silvestres cuyo futuro me importa y que trato de proteger, aunque quizás nunca los vea en persona. |
| RV6 | `rv_other` | Humans have a responsibility to account for our own impacts to the environment because they can harm other people. | Los seres humanos tenemos la responsabilidad de hacernos cargo de nuestros impactos en el ambiente, porque pueden hacer daño a otras personas. |

- **Response format:** 5-point Likert (1 = *Totalmente en desacuerdo* / Strongly disagree … 5 = *Totalmente de acuerdo* / Strongly agree), as in the original. Read aloud by the enumerator, with a show card on the tablet.
- **Scoring rules:** Mean of RV1–RV6 (range 1–5; higher = stronger relational values). No reverse-coded items. A score is computed if at least 5 of 6 items are answered; otherwise it is missing.
- **Known reliability:** Cronbach's α = .80 (full data set, N = 913): .73 among Costa Rican farmers, .79 among tourists in Costa Rica and .79 on M-Turk (Klain et al., 2017, Table 4). I will report α and McDonald's ω for the pilot and main samples.
- **Validation notes:** Klain et al. (2017) showed the six statements cluster as one index that is distinct from the NEP. Farmers agreed strongly (mean 4.4), so ceiling effects are a risk in rural Galápagos. The pilot will check the response distribution; a one-factor CFA is fitted in the main sample.
- **Fairness / cross-group equivalence considerations:**
  - The closest validated sample is rural Latin American (Costa Rican farmers), which supports use in the Galápagos highlands.
  - Cognitive interviews will probe *"kin"/familia*, which may read as literal or religious, and *"lugares silvestres"* (wild places), which may be read as the National Park specifically.
  - In the main sample, measurement invariance is tested across origin only (island-born vs. mainland-born; see `scale_notes.md`, section 4). Area and occupation are covariates.
- **Role in this study:** Focal predictor of intentions (Model 1) and attendance (Model 2) (H1), and part of the RV × trust interaction (H3, the confirmatory moderation). Measured once, in the single survey (stable characteristic).
