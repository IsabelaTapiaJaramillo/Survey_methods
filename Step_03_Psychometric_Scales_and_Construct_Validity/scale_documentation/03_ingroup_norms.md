# Scale documentation — Perceived In-group Norms (NORM)

*Status: **adapted** measure (the Step 03 table has no norm scale; see `../notes.md`).*

- **Construct name:** Perceived in-group norms about tortoise conservation: what people in the respondent's community are seen to **do** (descriptive norm) and to **approve of** (injunctive norm) (Cialdini et al., 1990; Fritsche et al., 2018).
- **Source / citation:** Adapted from norm items used in SIMPEA studies (e.g., Johnson & Reimer, 2023; *full reference to be completed*), following SIMPEA's definition of in-group norms. The wording is tailored to giant tortoise conservation on Santa Cruz.
- **Items (numbered):**

| # | Variable | Norm type | English | Spanish draft |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| NORM1 | `norm_inj` | Injunctive (group values) | Most people in my community think it is important to protect giant tortoises. | La mayoría de las personas de mi comunidad piensa que es importante proteger a las tortugas gigantes. |
| NORM2 | `norm_desc` | Descriptive (group behavior) | Most people in my community take part in activities to care for giant tortoises. | La mayoría de las personas de mi comunidad participa en actividades para cuidar a las tortugas gigantes. |
| NORM3 | `norm_approve` | Injunctive (approval of me) | People in my community would approve of me taking part in tortoise conservation activities. | Las personas de mi comunidad verían bien que yo participe en actividades de conservación de tortugas. |

- **Response format:** 5-point Likert (1 = Strongly disagree … 5 = Strongly agree).
- **Scoring rules:** Mean of NORM1–NORM3 (range 1–5), computed if at least 2 of 3 items are answered. Because descriptive and injunctive norms can diverge, NORM2 (descriptive) is also reported on its own.
- **Known reliability:** Not yet established in this population. The target in the pilot is α/ω ≥ .70. With 3 items, ω is preferred over α.
- **Validation notes:**
  - In the pilot, I will fit a two-factor CFA (norms vs. collective efficacy, 3 + 3 items) to show that the two constructs are distinct (discriminant validity, HTMT < .85).
  - "Mi comunidad" is defined at the start of the block as *"las personas que viven en Santa Cruz"* (the people who live on Santa Cruz), so that it refers to the same in-group as ID1–ID4.
- **Fairness / cross-group equivalence considerations:** Highland farmers may think of their parish, and Puerto Ayora residents of their neighborhood. Cognitive interviews will check which group people picture. The prompt anchors the group to Santa Cruz.
- **Role in this study:** Moderator in H1c (ID × norms) and **outcome** in H2b (expected to rise after the festival). Measured **before and after**.
