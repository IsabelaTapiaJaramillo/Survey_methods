# Scale documentation — Collective Efficacy (CE)

*Status: **adapted** measure (the Step 03 table has no collective-efficacy scale; see `../notes.md`).*

- **Construct name:** Collective efficacy: the shared belief that residents of Santa Cruz, acting together, can achieve tortoise conservation goals (Bandura, 2000; Fritsche et al., 2018).
- **Source / citation:** Adapted from collective-efficacy items in SIMPEA studies (e.g., Johnson & Reimer, 2023). The items follow SIMPEA's "we can do it together" definition and are tailored to tortoise conservation.
- **Items (numbered):**

| # | Variable | English | Spanish draft |
|---|---|---|---|
| CE1 | `ce_difference` | Together, the people of Santa Cruz can make a real difference for giant tortoises. | Juntos, los habitantes de Santa Cruz podemos marcar una diferencia real para las tortugas gigantes. |
| CE2 | `ce_habitat` | If people in my community work together, we can protect the places where tortoises live on the island. | Si las personas de mi comunidad trabajamos juntas, podemos proteger los lugares donde viven las tortugas en la isla. |
| CE3 | `ce_capacity` | The people of Santa Cruz have the knowledge and skills needed to help conserve giant tortoises. | Los habitantes de Santa Cruz tenemos los conocimientos y las capacidades necesarias para ayudar a conservar a las tortugas gigantes. |

- **Response format:** 5-point Likert (1 = Strongly disagree … 5 = Strongly agree).
- **Scoring rules:** Mean of CE1–CE3 (range 1–5), computed if at least 2 of 3 items are answered.
- **Known reliability:** Not yet established in this population. The target in the pilot is ω ≥ .70.
- **Validation notes:**
  - Discriminant validity against in-group norms is tested in the two-factor CFA (see `03_ingroup_norms.md`).
  - Convergent validity: CE should correlate positively with intentions (cf. Fritsche et al., 2018).
  - CE3 ("knowledge and skills") targets the festival's portrait exhibition, which shows residents as capable protectors.
- **Fairness / cross-group equivalence considerations:** Groups that historically had conflicts with park rules (e.g., some fishers and farmers) may separate "we residents can" from "we are allowed to". Cognitive interviews will probe whether CE items are read as being about *ability* or about *permission*.
- **Role in this study:** **Outcome** in H2b (before and after).
